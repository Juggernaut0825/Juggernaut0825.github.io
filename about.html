About

I'm Zijian (Michael) Wang, an M.S. in Statistics student at the Georgia Institute of Technology. I hold a B.S. in Quantitative Sciences (Data Science track) with a minor in Computer Science from Emory University. I like building AI systems that real people use, and I like measuring them carefully enough to know whether they actually work.

Right now I'm collaborating with Microsoft Research mentors on IntentDelta, a benchmark that tests whether tool-calling LLM agents keep up when a user's goals change mid-conversation. Before that, as a Data Science & AI Engineer intern at E Closet, I shipped a six-tier semantic search engine, a personalized recommendation system, FashionSigLIP-based reverse-image search, and Ask-e, a 14-tool AI shopping assistant, all serving production traffic. At Buildsense AI, I built PaperMem, an explainable memory system for agents that combines pgvector retrieval with Neo4j graph expansion.

My statistics background shapes how I build. My honors thesis, "Minimax NTK in Estimating Average Treatment Effect Confounded by Image Covariate" (advised by Dr. David Hirshberg), developed a Neural Tangent Kernel balancing estimator for causal inference with high-dimensional image confounders. I bring that same rigor to offline evals, A/B-style comparisons, and benchmark design.

On the side, I build ZYM, an AI personal-trainer app for iOS and web with 400+ users, featuring a multimodal agent and a fine-tuned barbell-tracking detector. Earlier work spans sports analytics (Atlanta Braves, Golden State Warriors), NLP, and environmental-justice GIS work with Science for Georgia. See the portfolio for more.

Outside of work: basketball (lifelong Warriors fan), jazz saxophone, lifting, and hiking. I'm open to relocation and always happy to chat about agents, retrieval, or evaluation.
